Data We Collect

My Game Room does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information from its users.

The only data stored by the app is a list of games marked as favorites by the user. This data is stored exclusively on the user's device (local storage) using Android's Room database. It is never sent to any external server, never shared with third parties, and never used for analytics or advertising purposes.

Third-party API connections

My Game Room connects to the IGDB API (operated by Twitch Interactive, Inc.) to retrieve publicly available video game information such as titles, cover images, ratings, and genres. This connection is made to display game data within the app.

When the app connects to the IGDB API, standard network request information (such as IP address) may be processed by Twitch as part of their service. We do not control this processing. You can review Twitch's Privacy Policy at: https://www.twitch.tv/p/en/legal/privacy-notice/

No personal data entered by the user is sent to IGDB or any other third party.

How We Use Information

Since we do not collect personal data, we do not use it for any purpose. The app functions entirely based on publicly available game data retrieved from IGDB and locally stored user preferences (favorites).

Data Retention

Favorite games selected by the user are stored locally on the device until the user removes them or uninstalls the application. We have no access to this data at any time.

Children's Privacy

Our Service does not address anyone under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from anyone under the age of 16. If You are a parent or guardian and believe Your child has provided personal information through our app, please contact Us so We can take appropriate action.

Security

Since My Game Room does not collect or transmit personal data, there is no personal data at risk of being intercepted or breached through our systems. The locally stored favorites list remains on your device and is protected by your device's own security mechanisms.

Your Rights Under the GDPR (European Union)

As a developer based in Spain and operating within the European Union, We are committed to complying with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR — Regulation EU 2016/679).

Since My Game Room does not collect or process any personal data from its users, most GDPR rights are not applicable in practice. However, We acknowledge the following rights that EU residents hold:

  • Right of access: You have the right to request information about any personal data We hold about You.
  • Right to rectification: You have the right to request correction of inaccurate personal data.
  • Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten"): You have the right to request deletion of Your personal data.
  • Right to restriction of processing: You have the right to request that We restrict the processing of Your personal data.
  • Right to data portability: You have the right to receive Your personal data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Right to object: You have the right to object to the processing of Your personal data.

Since the app only stores data locally on Your own device, You can exercise Your right to erasure at any time by removing favorites within the app or uninstalling the application. For any other requests or questions regarding these rights, please contact Us at kodarisu.dev@gmail.com.
